The short version

Clients pay when a room serves them. Upgrade and hire so they don’t walk out. Managers are permanent. Goals, VIP clients and home auctions are the big paydays. Move town once the office is maxed. Diamonds, managers, VIP and your level come with you. Cash and rooms do not.

A purchase didn’t unlock

In the iOS app, nothing is granted unless Apple confirms the charge. If the App Store is slow, the game shows an error and leaves your wallet alone. Open Settings, then Restore purchases, on the same Apple ID you paid with. Consumable diamond packs are not restored. VIP, ad-free, the Starter Pack and manager packs are.

On the website, purchases are still a test and are granted on the spot. That path is not the App Store.

Ads

A rewarded video is optional: double offline earnings, double an auction commission, double a level-up, skip a Board Room wait, double a goal, or a free shop payout (three a day). If the video fails or you close it, you keep the ordinary reward where one was already on the table, and you don’t get the double. VIP clients are the exception we kept on purpose: turning one away still costs reputation.

Saw a bad ad? Report it. Include the time and what was on screen.
