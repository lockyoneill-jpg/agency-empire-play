Support

The office runs on your device. These are the fixes that don’t need a ticket, and the address for the ones that do.

A purchase didn’t show up

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. On iPhone that asks Apple, and on Android it asks Google Play, for non-consumable items already bought: VIP, ad-free, the starter pack, and manager packs. Diamonds are consumable and are not restored. If the store doesn’t answer, nothing is granted for free and you aren’t charged again.

Report an ad

If an ad is broken, offensive, or won’t close, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER@example.com with the time of day and what the ad was for. The same link sits in Settings.

Reminders

Office reminders are off until you turn them on. They are local notifications for offline earnings and for a quiet office, not messages from us. Settings → Office reminders, or the phone’s notification settings for Agency Empire.

Saves

There is no cloud save. The iOS and Android apps keep a second copy in app storage. Resetting the game in Settings wipes both. Privacy policy.

Contact

SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER@example.com is a placeholder inbox. Replace it in the app config and on this page before the App Store or Play listing goes live.
