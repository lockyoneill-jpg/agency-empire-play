Privacy policy

Agency Empire, from Studio Den. Last updated 28 October 2026.

Your town stays on this device

Cash, rooms, managers and the rest of your save live in storage on the phone or browser. On the iOS app a second copy is kept in the app’s own storage, so iOS can clear the web copy without wiping the agency. There is no account and no cloud save. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site’s data, removes the save.

Purchases

VIP, ad-free, diamonds, the Starter Pack and manager packs are in-app purchases sold by Apple. Apple charges you and tells the app what you bought, so the game can unlock it. We never see your card number. Restoring purchases asks Apple for the non-consumable items already on that Apple ID. Prices in the game are shown in Australian dollars until the App Store sends its own price.

Advertising

The iOS app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners can receive a device identifier, IP address, coarse location, and how you interact with an ad, so they can serve and measure ads. While the game is still on Google’s test ad units, those ads are samples, not a live campaign. Rewarded ads are optional. A full-screen break can appear after you close an auction result or move town. It does not play for VIP or ad-free players, during the tutorial, in the first two towns, or while a window is open.

The tracking prompt

On iPhone the app asks, once, whether it may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. That question is Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. It shows up during ordinary play, not jammed against an ad. If you say no, the game keeps working and ads can still appear, just less tailored. You can change the answer later in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Consent in the EU and UK

Before the first ad, the app asks Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) where the law requires it, including the EU and the UK. You can accept, refuse, or manage choices there. Ads are requested only after that step has had a chance to run.

Report an ad

If an ad is offensive, broken, or aimed at children, email support@studioden.digital with the rough time and what you saw. That address is a placeholder until the studio mailbox is live. The same link is in Settings.

Children

Agency Empire is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@studioden.digital. More help is on the support page.
