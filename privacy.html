Agency Empire

Privacy policy

This policy covers the Agency Empire app and the web version of the same game. It is written for players, not for a filing cabinet. The contact address below is a placeholder until the studio publishes the live one.

Saves stay on your device

Progress is stored locally on the phone or browser (web storage, and on the iOS app a second copy in the app’s own storage so iOS can clear a web cache without wiping the office). There is no game account and no studio server that receives your save. Uninstalling the app, or resetting it in Settings, deletes that copy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google and its partners can collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, and use them to deliver and measure ads. Google’s own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Buying Ad-free or VIP + No ads turns ads off. Rewards that used to sit behind an ad are then paid without one.

Tracking permission

On iPhone the app may ask for permission to track you across apps and websites. That is Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. It is used so ads can be more relevant. The game plays the same if you tap Ask App Not to Track. The prompt is shown at a quiet moment, not jammed against an ad.

Consent in the EU and UK

Before ads are requested, the app asks Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether a consent form is required. In the EU, the UK, and other regions where that form applies, you can accept, refuse, or manage ad personalisation. Ads are only requested when Google says they may be. You can reopen ad privacy choices from the iOS app when Google marks that option as required.

Purchases

Paid items are in-app purchases processed by Apple through StoreKit. The studio does not receive your card number or billing address. Apple’s records are what Restore Purchases checks. Prices shown in the shop are in Australian dollars until the App Store substitutes your local price.

Reminders

If you opt in, the iOS app schedules local notifications on the device: one while offline earnings are building, one when the offline till is full, and a later nudge if the office has gone idle. They are not sent by a server. You can turn them off in Settings inside the game, and iOS can revoke the permission.

Share

Share buttons open the iOS share sheet (or the browser’s share sheet). Whatever you send goes to the app you pick. The game does not receive a copy.

Contact

Privacy questions and ad reports: support@studioden.digital. Replace this address before release if the studio uses a different inbox. See also the support page.
